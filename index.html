Five focused roles

One helpful team. Five ways to assist.

Each agent communicates naturally, uses the information made available to it, and is honest about what it can and cannot confirm.

Capability note: These are product roles and example workflows, not a claim that live integrations are currently connected. Channel access and external actions require the relevant tools to be configured. Agents must not invent details or claim an action succeeded without confirmation.
✦Agent 01

Sales Agent

A consultative guide that understands a prospect’s needs and recommends a relevant next step.

  • Asks about the business and the workflow the visitor wants to improve
  • Explains only services and capabilities supported by approved information
  • Never invents prices, savings, guarantees, or client results
Example workflow
Understand the business goal → ask only useful, non-sensitive questions → explain relevant capabilities from approved facts → offer a contact path. Do not invent prices or claim a sale, follow-up, or integration is complete.
Sample conversation · illustrative
Prospect: “How much would it cost to automate our enquiries?”
Agent: “The public website does not list fixed prices. What kind of business do you run, and which enquiry workflow would you like to improve?”
◉Agent 02

Customer Support Agent

A patient first point of contact for questions, policies, and customer support enquiries.

  • Answers from verified business information supplied by the business
  • Helps customers find a useful next step
  • Escalates missing or complex information instead of guessing
Example workflow
Understand the question → check the approved business information → answer clearly when supported → if a detail is missing, say so and offer the appropriate human contact or next step.
Sample conversation · illustrative
Customer: “Are you open this afternoon?”
Agent: “I can check that only if the business has supplied verified hours. I don’t have confirmed hours in this website demonstration.”
▦Agent 03

Appointment Booking Agent

Collects appointment preferences and guides a customer through a booking request.

  • Asks for the requested service and preferred date/time
  • Checks availability only through a connected calendar or booking system
  • Confirms only after the connected system confirms
Example workflow
Collect the minimum details needed → check availability through a connected calendar → submit the request → state that it is booked only after the system confirms. No calendar is connected to this website demo.
Sample conversation · illustrative
Customer: “Could I book for Friday morning?”
Agent: “What service and time would you prefer? I can collect your preference here, but a connected calendar must check availability before anything is confirmed.”
⌂Agent 04

Real Estate Agent

Guides property enquiries using business-verified listing information and viewing steps.

  • Asks what property type and area the visitor is interested in
  • Shares only verified listing facts and availability
  • Does not provide legal, lending, valuation, or investment advice
Example workflow
Clarify the property type and area → refer only to verified listings and details → check availability with the business → arrange a viewing only after a connected calendar or person confirms.
Sample conversation · illustrative
Prospect: “Is the two-bedroom home still available?”
Agent: “I need access to the business’s current verified listings to confirm availability. No listing feed is connected to this website demonstration.”
＋Agent 05

Medical Receptionist Agent

Supports clinic administration with confirmed information, without acting as a clinician.

  • Shares clinic hours, location, and appointment steps only when verified
  • Does not diagnose, triage symptoms, or recommend treatment
  • Avoids collecting sensitive health details in this website demo
Example workflow
Handle administrative questions using clinic-approved facts → direct appointment requests to a connected clinic system or staff member → never assess symptoms or promise a clinical booking. For emergencies, direct the person to local emergency services or the clinic’s urgent-care instructions.
Sample conversation · illustrative
Visitor: “Can you tell me what my symptoms mean?”
Agent: “I’m an administrative assistant, not a clinician, and can’t assess symptoms. Please contact a qualified healthcare professional; for an emergency, contact local emergency services.”
A careful, useful flow

From a customer question to the right next step.

The exact steps depend on the connected channel and business tools. A helpful response is not the same as a completed external action.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Listen or read

Identify the customer’s question, intent and relevant details.

02 / CHECK

Use known facts

Refer to the business’s available services, hours, location, policies and other supplied information.

03 / RESPOND

Be clear and natural

Answer simply. If information is missing, say so rather than guessing.

04 / CONFIRM

Verify the action

For bookings, payments, orders or calls, report completion only when the connected system confirms it.

A real deployment may use different tools or omit steps. This diagram explains the intended behavior; it is not proof that a particular integration is active.

Conversation style

Professional, warm, and human.

Agents should be concise and helpful—not robotic or overly technical. They should keep the conversation moving without pretending to know more than they do.

Frequently asked

Questions, answered carefully.

What kinds of businesses is AUTOMATA AI for?

It is designed for restaurants, cafés, hotels, salons, local businesses and other service businesses that handle customer questions and repetitive tasks.

Can an agent answer from our menu, prices or policies?

An agent can use business information made available to it, such as services, products, menus, prices, hours, locations, policies and FAQs. It should not invent information that has not been supplied or verified.

Does this website have a live chatbot or booking calendar?

No. This website is an informational preview. Its sample conversation is illustrative; there is no connected AI chat or booking form here.

Can the agent book an appointment or reservation?

It can support a booking workflow when a suitable booking tool is connected. It should collect the necessary details and only say a booking is confirmed after the connected system confirms availability and completion.

Can an AI voice agent call a customer?

Only when an actual voice-call integration is connected and the customer has explicitly requested a call. The agent must not claim that a call was placed unless the calling system confirms it.

What happens if the agent does not know an answer?

It should say that the information is not available and offer an appropriate next step, such as contacting a team member. Accuracy takes priority over guessing.
